# EN 15804 (2012+A2:2019)

EN 15804:2012+A2:2019, *Sustainability of construction works, Environmental product
declarations, Core rules for the product category of construction products*, is the
European standard that sets the core product category rules for construction product
EPDs. It is published by CEN and is paywalled (en-15804-2019). Amendment A2 replaced the
earlier A1:2013 amendment; programme rules built on it reference the corrected version
EN 15804:2012+A2:2019/AC:2021 (epd-pcr-2019-14, section 1.3).

This page describes the module structure and the requirements as a programme operator's
public product category rules state them; it does not reproduce the standard's text.

## Scope

EN 15804 covers construction products and, through programme rules, construction
services. It is a core PCR: it is used together with a product category rule document
from a programme operator, and complementary PCRs (c-PCRs) narrow it further for specific
product groups. An EPD based on the core rules plus a programme PCR alone uses a
*declared unit*; a *functional unit* is required for a cradle-to-grave and module D EPD,
and an EPD with a functional unit needs a c-PCR
(epd-pcr-2019-14, sections 1.2, 1.4 and 4.1).

## What it requires

### The information modules

The life cycle of a construction product is divided into life cycle stages and
information modules (epd-pcr-2019-14, Table 2):

| Stage | Modules |
|---|---|
| A1 to A3, product stage | A1 raw material supply, A2 transport, A3 manufacturing |
| A4 to A5, construction process stage | A4 transport, A5 construction installation |
| B, use stage | B1 use, B2 maintenance, B3 repair, B4 replacement, B5 refurbishment, B6 operational energy use, B7 operational water use |
| C, end of life stage | C1 deconstruction and demolition, C2 transport, C3 waste processing, C4 disposal |
| D, beyond the system boundary | D reuse, recovery, recycling potential |

### Which modules are mandatory

EN 15804 section 5.2 allows several EPD types, and the programme rules tabulate which
modules each one must declare (epd-pcr-2019-14, sections 2.2.2 and Table 2):

- a) Cradle to gate with modules C1 to C4 and module D (A1 to A3 + C + D).
- b) Cradle to gate with options, modules C1 to C4, module D and optional modules, which
  may be selected from A4 to A5 and B1 to B7.
- c) Cradle to grave and module D (A + B + C + D), which uses a functional unit.
- d) and e) Cradle to gate, and cradle to gate with options, where an EPD may exclude
  C1 to C4 and D only if the stated conditions are met.
- f) Construction service EPD: cradle to gate with modules A1 to A5 and optional modules.

In practice this means modules A1 to A3, C1 to C4 and module D form the normal minimum
scope for a construction product EPD (epd-pcr-2019-14, Table 2).

### Module D

Module D reports the net benefits and loads beyond the system boundary from reuse,
recovery and recycling. It is mandatory for EPD types a, b and c and is not applicable to
a construction service EPD (epd-pcr-2019-14, Table 2). Because it is defined as beyond
the system boundary, its results are declared separately and are not added into the
product result: this is the same distinction that
[system-boundary.md](../concepts/system-boundary.md) draws in general terms.

### Declared unit versus functional unit

A declared unit is used where the function and the reference scenario for the whole life
cycle of the construction works are not stated; a functional unit is used where they are,
and is required for a cradle-to-grave EPD (epd-pcr-2019-14, sections 4.1 and Table 2).
Two EPDs that use declared units are therefore not automatically comparable; the
comparison only holds at building level.

## How it relates to other documents

- **ISO 14040 and 14044.** EN 15804 applies LCA as standardised there, with the modules as
  a prescribed system boundary structure. See [iso-14040-14044.md](iso-14040-14044.md).
- **ISO 21930.** The international counterpart for construction product EPDs. Programme
  rules note that EN 15804 and ISO 21930 assign some processes, such as electricity
  generation, to different modules, which is why results for A1 to A3 are declared in
  aggregated form so that one EPD can comply with both, and that their allocation
  requirements for production scrap from A1 to A3 are not fully compatible
  (epd-pcr-2019-14, sections 1.4 and 5.4.5).
- **Programme PCRs and c-PCRs.** The core rules are always used with a programme
  operator's PCR; PCR 2019:14 *Construction products* is one such document and states
  that all EPDs based on it comply with EN 15804:2012+A2:2019/AC:2021
  (epd-pcr-2019-14, sections 1.2 and 1.3).
- **EF/PEF.** A separate European system with its own category rules and impact category
  set; see [ef-pef.md](ef-pef.md).
