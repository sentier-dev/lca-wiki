# ILCD Handbook (2010)

The International Reference Life Cycle Data System (ILCD) Handbook is a series of
technical documents published by the European Commission's Joint Research Centre,
Institute for Environment and Sustainability. The document this wiki treats as its
reference is the *General guide for Life Cycle Assessment, Detailed guidance*, first
edition, March 2010, EUR 24708 EN (ilcd-2010).

The ILCD was developed because the ISO framework, while indispensable, leaves the
practitioner a range of choices that can affect the legitimacy of an LCA's results;
further guidance was needed to support consistency and quality assurance
(ilcd-2010, "About the International Reference Life Cycle Data System"). The ILCD
consists primarily of the ILCD Handbook and the ILCD Data Network.

## Scope

The general guide provides detailed guidance for planning, developing and reporting both
life cycle inventory data sets and LCA studies. Its principal audience is the LCA
practitioner and technical experts in the public and private sector working on
environmental decision support for products, resources and waste management
(ilcd-2010, "About this guide" and "Role of this document").

Within the handbook series, this document has the role of providing the general,
overarching guidance. It is complemented by specific guides on the development of LCI
data sets, on the development of LCIA models and indicators, and on performing reviews of
LCI data sets, LCA studies and of specific guides and simplified approaches. It is
further supported by an LCA study report template, an LCI data set documentation format,
a document on nomenclature and other conventions, and a terminology, all available
separately (ilcd-2010, "Role of this document within the ILCD Handbook").

The handbook is explicitly not a comprehensive introduction or a training manual for
beginners (ilcd-2010, "Role of this document"). It also serves as a parent document for
developing sector and product-group specific guidance, criteria and simplified
ecodesign-type tools (ilcd-2010, "About the ILCD").

## What it requires

The handbook states its requirements as numbered "Provisions" at the end of each chapter,
also issued separately in a cook-book style guide for experienced practitioners and
reviewers (ilcd-2010, "Role of this document"). Provisions are marked to show whether
they restate ISO or go beyond it. The main things the handbook fixes that ISO leaves
open:

- **Three decision contexts.** Every study is classified as Situation A, "micro-level
  decision support", where decisions have limited and no structural consequences outside
  the decision context; Situation B, "meso/macro-level decision support", where decisions
  are assumed to change available production capacity; or Situation C, "accounting",
  purely descriptive documentation without interest in consequences elsewhere
  (ilcd-2010, "Approach taken" and chapter 5.3). Chapter 5.3 gives explicit guidance for
  differentiating A from B and C from A/B.
- **LCI modelling provisions per situation.** Chapter 6.5.4 states which modelling
  principle and which multifunctionality solution apply in each situation, filling the
  gap the handbook identifies in ISO 14044, which does not address the LCI modelling
  framework in any detail (ilcd-2010, chapters 6.5.2 and 6.5.4).
- **Six aspects of the goal definition.** Intended applications; method, assumption and
  impact limitations; reasons and decision context; target audience; whether comparisons
  are disclosed to the public; and the commissioner and other influential actors
  (ilcd-2010, chapter 5.2).
- **Data quality as six indicators.** Technological, geographical and time-related
  representativeness, completeness, precision or uncertainty, and methodological
  appropriateness and consistency, with quality levels and ratings in Annex A
  (ilcd-2010, chapter 12.3).
- **A quantitative cut-off concept.** Completeness expressed as a share of environmental
  impact, either per impact category or against the normalised and weighted overall
  impact (ilcd-2010, chapter 6.6.3).
- **Unit process typology.** "Unit process, single operation" versus "unit process, black
  box", introduced so that the distorting effect of allocating black boxes can be named
  (ilcd-2010, chapters 3 and 6.5.3.2).
- **Three levels of reporting.** An internal report, a third party report, and a report on
  comparative studies disclosed to the public (ilcd-2010, chapter 10.3).
- **Annexes for the hard cases.** Data quality concept and indicators (Annex A), CO2 from
  land transformation (Annex B), reuse, recycling and energy recovery (Annex C),
  misleading goal and scope definition and interpretation (Annex D), uncertainties
  (Annex E), and a system boundary template (Annex F)
  (ilcd-2010, chapters 12 to 17).

The handbook also states what to do when it is itself unclear: chapter 2.4 covers dealing
with potential omissions and contradictions within the ILCD Handbook
(ilcd-2010, chapter 2.4), and chapter 2.3 defines ILCD compliance and the role of the
provisions.

## How it relates to other documents

- **ISO 14040 and 14044.** The guide is based on and conforms to them, and further details
  the ISO 14044 provisions (ilcd-2010, "About this guide" and "Approach taken"). Where a
  provision is ISO-derived it is marked as such; where the handbook adds, it is marked as
  going beyond. See [iso-14040-14044.md](iso-14040-14044.md).
- **The ILCD data format.** The LCI data set documentation format is one of the supporting
  documents of the handbook series (ilcd-2010, "Role of this document"). See
  [ilcd-format.md](ilcd-format.md).
- **Nomenclature and other conventions.** A separate handbook document defines the default
  compartments and naming rules that the general guide refers to
  (ilcd-2010, chapters 7.4.5 and 8.2). See
  [glad-and-nomenclatures.md](glad-and-nomenclatures.md).
- **EF/PEF.** The Environmental Footprint methods and the EF-compliant data set rules are
  built on the ILCD format and on the ILCD entry-level compliance scheme. See
  [ef-pef.md](ef-pef.md).
