# ISO 14040 and ISO 14044 (2006, amended 2020)

ISO 14040:2006, *Environmental management, Life cycle assessment, Principles and
framework*, and ISO 14044:2006, *Environmental management, Life cycle assessment,
Requirements and guidelines*, are the pair of International Standards that define what an
LCA is. They were published by ISO in 2006 and replaced the earlier ISO 14040 to 14043
series. Both were amended in 2020: ISO 14040:2006/Amd 1:2020 and ISO 14044:2006/Amd
2:2020 were published on 24 September 2020 by ISO/TC 207/SC 5, Life cycle assessment,
and both stand at stage "60.60 Standard published"
(iso-14040-2006-amd1-2020, iso-14044-2006-amd2-2020).

The standards are paywalled. This page describes what they require and where; it does not
reproduce their text.

## Scope

ISO 14040 gives the principles and the framework: the four phases (goal and scope
definition, inventory analysis, impact assessment, interpretation), the life cycle
perspective, the relative approach anchored in a functional unit, the iterative nature of
the method, transparency, comprehensiveness, and the priority of the scientific approach.
It is descriptive rather than prescriptive: it does not by itself state requirements a
study must meet.

ISO 14044 carries the requirements and guidelines. It is the clause structure that every
LCA methodology document, including the ILCD Handbook, cross-references
(ilcd-2010, chapters 5 to 10).

What neither standard does is choose for the practitioner. The ILCD Handbook's own
rationale says this plainly: the ISO framework leaves the individual practitioner a range
of choices that can affect the legitimacy of the results, and further guidance is needed
to support consistency and quality assurance (ilcd-2010, "About the International
Reference Life Cycle Data System"). One concrete gap the handbook names: while
multifunctionality is well covered in ISO 14044, the more fundamental question of the
appropriate LCI modelling framework is not addressed in any detail and has no
corresponding ISO chapter (ilcd-2010, chapter 6.5.2, footnote).

## What it requires

The clause references below are the ones the ILCD Handbook cites when it refers back to
ISO (ilcd-2010, chapter cross-references).

- **Goal definition.** State the intended application, the reasons for the study, the
  intended audience, and whether the results are intended for comparative assertions
  disclosed to the public (ISO 14044 clause 4.2.2; ilcd-2010, chapter 5).
- **Scope: function, functional unit, reference flow.** ISO 14044 clause 4.2.3.2 and
  aspects of 4.2.3.3.1 (ilcd-2010, chapter 6.4).
- **Scope: system boundary.** ISO 14044 clause 4.2.3.3, with the cut-off criteria in
  4.2.3.3.3, which requires the criteria to be stated and their effect documented
  (ilcd-2010, chapter 6.6).
- **Scope: data quality requirements.** ISO 14044 clauses 4.2.3.6 and 4.3.2.1, covering
  time-related, geographical and technological coverage, precision, completeness,
  representativeness, consistency, reproducibility, sources and uncertainty
  (ilcd-2010, chapters 12.2 and 12.3).
- **Inventory analysis.** Data collection, validation, relating data to the unit process
  and the functional unit, and refining the system boundary; ISO 14044 clause 4.3
  (ilcd-2010, chapter 7).
- **Allocation.** The hierarchy of avoiding allocation by subdivision or system expansion,
  then partitioning by a physical relationship, then by another relationship, presented
  in ISO 14044 under the heading "Allocation"
  (ilcd-2010, chapter 6.5.3.2).
- **Impact assessment.** Mandatory elements of selection, classification and
  characterisation; optional elements of normalisation (clause 4.4.3.2), grouping and
  weighting (clause 4.4.3.4) (ilcd-2010, chapters 8.2 to 8.4).
- **The weighting prohibition.** Under ISO 14040 and 14044, no form of numerical,
  value-based weighting of the indicator results may be published for a study intended to
  support a comparative assertion disclosed to the public
  (ilcd-2010, chapters 8.2 and 8.4).
- **Interpretation.** Identification of significant issues; evaluation by completeness,
  sensitivity and consistency checks; conclusions, limitations and recommendations;
  ISO 14044 clause 4.5 (ilcd-2010, chapter 9).
- **Reporting and critical review.** Reporting requirements, and a critical review by
  interested parties for comparative assertions disclosed to the public
  (ilcd-2010, chapters 10 and 11).

The 2020 amendments are short documents of a few pages each and are paywalled; this wiki
records that they exist, their designations and their publication date, and does not
paraphrase their content, which could not be read from a public source
(iso-14040-2006-amd1-2020, iso-14044-2006-amd2-2020).

## How it relates to other documents

- **ILCD Handbook.** The general guide is based on and conforms to ISO 14040 and 14044,
  and further details the ISO 14044 provisions, differentiating them for three types of
  question: micro-level decision support, meso/macro-level decision support, and
  accounting (ilcd-2010, "Role of this document within the ILCD Handbook" and "Approach
  taken"). Its provisions are marked to show which derive from ISO and which go beyond
  it.
- **EF/PEF.** The Environmental Footprint methods build on ISO 14040/44 and narrow them
  to a fixed method; see [ef-pef.md](ef-pef.md).
- **EN 15804.** The construction EPD standard applies ISO 14040/44 through a module
  structure and a declared unit; see [en-15804.md](en-15804.md).
- **ILCD data format.** The Process data set is described as covering the ISO/TS 14048
  process description, so the exchange format follows a different but compatible ISO
  document; see [ilcd-format.md](ilcd-format.md).
