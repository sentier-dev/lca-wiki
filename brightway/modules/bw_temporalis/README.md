# bw_temporalis

`bw_temporalis` gives a Brightway inventory a time axis. An exchange carries a
`TemporalDistribution` saying when its amount happens relative to the process consuming
it; a graph traversal convolves those distributions along each path; and the result is
a timeline of dated flows that can be characterised with a function of time rather than
a single factor.

It is the revision of the older `bw2temporalis` for modern Brightway
(source: pypi-bw-temporalis). It is also the layer `bw_timex` builds on: where
`bw_temporalis` dates the flows inside one database, `bw_timex` additionally relinks
processes to background databases of different vintages. See
[../bw_timex/README.md](../bw_timex/README.md).

## What it does

- **Describes when something happens.** `TemporalDistribution` holds an array of dates
  or offsets and an array of amounts. `FixedTD` and `FixedTimeOfYearTD` express
  absolute timing. See [classes.md](classes.md).
- **Builds distributions without arrays.** `easy_timedelta_distribution` and
  `easy_datetime_distribution` construct uniform, triangular or normal shapes from a
  few parameters. See [classes.md](classes.md).
- **Traverses with time.** `TemporalisLCA` subclasses the `bw_graph_tools` traversal
  and convolves the distributions along each path. See [classes.md](classes.md).
- **Collects the result.** `Timeline` sums and groups the dated flows, ready to
  characterise or plot. See [classes.md](classes.md).
- **Characterises dynamically.** The `lcia` subpackage ships radiative forcing
  functions for carbon dioxide and methane. See [datasets.md](datasets.md).

## Datasets

No data files. The installed wheel is Python modules only: no flow list, no
nomenclature, no impact assessment method in the Brightway sense. What it does ship are
two characterisation *functions*, for carbon dioxide and methane radiative forcing, and
a registry that lets a temporal distribution be serialised into and out of a Brightway
database. [datasets.md](datasets.md) has the detail.
